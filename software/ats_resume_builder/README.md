# ATS Analyzer and Resume Builder

Paste a designation, company, job description, and resume. The tool:

1. Reads the job post and your resume
2. Lists matched skills vs gaps
3. Gives an ATS-style score (this tool, not Workday/Greenhouse themselves)
4. Builds a plain-text resume aimed at 90+ when job keywords are mirrored
5. Downloads `.txt`, `.html`, or `.docx`

Do not keep suggested skills unless they are true.

Future GitHub repo: `ats-resume-builder`. Live on the portfolio at `/apps/ats`.
